Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 | Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

At Haven Real Estate Technologies, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. We collect personal details when you register as a Client or Real Estate Agent, including your full name, email address, phone number, property preferences, and real estate license credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data
  • Facilitating seamless appointment scheduling between clients and real estate professionals.
  • Verifying real estate agent licenses and professional credentials.
  • Providing customized property recommendations and price filtering.
  • Ensuring secure authentication and fraud prevention.
3. Data Protection & Security

All sensitive user information and passwords are encrypted using one-way cryptographic hashing (BCrypt). We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for commercial advertising.

4. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to review, update, or request the deletion of your account profile and appointment history at any time through your dashboard or by contacting our administration team.
